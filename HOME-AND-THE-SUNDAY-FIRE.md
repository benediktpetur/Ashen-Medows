# Home and the Sunday fire

The three recruitable settlers have distinct authored family stories. Mara remembers her sister June and niece Elsie, lost at the evacuation. Ivar carries the key to the home he shared with Signe. Nora is still waiting for Daniel and Theo to return. Their later dialogue includes ordinary, specific joys and the guilt of feeling them. Missing relatives remain missing; the game does not pretend all losses have been resolved.

## Interactions

Stand within 4m of an able resident, press F and ask about family, feelings or their new home, or offer quiet company. These are also available through the selected resident in F7; the host still enforces physical range and safety. Family memories are recorded once. Quiet company grants 3 morale with a shared 10-active-minute cooldown. It does not erase grief or buy instant acceptance. Conversations are text; this pass does not add voiced acting.

F7 -> Camp stories opens the shared memory book, newest living arrival, belonging, remembrance and fireside transcript. Personal trust in the player remains the existing separate reputation system.

## Belonging

Recruitment assigns a persistent arrival sequence. Group acceptance begins at zero/neutral, even if the rescued settler trusts the player. With another able resident at home, food and a sheltered assigned bed, acceptance grows by about 21.6 points per active game day. Thresholds: 20 finding a place, 55 belongs here, 80 family by choice. Grieving residents grow at half speed. Acceptance contributes up to 8 points to the morale target. Absent, unhoused or unfed residents do not gain it automatically. Legacy members receive a stable arrival order; no invented lived history is granted.

## The gathering

The game calendar begins on Monday. Sunday evenings are 18:00-21:00. Once a communal hearth (camp improvement 2) exists, at least two able, unassigned residents at home can share a meal and talk. A welcome evening for the newest member can happen before Sunday if that newcomer is present. Each attendee consumes one camp food portion; starting again that day cannot duplicate meals or benefits. Speaking residents gain 4 morale and 4 acceptance. No research or items are awarded.

Residents walk to positions around the existing hearth, put tools away when gathered and face the fire. Authored dialogue appears as nearby subtitles, including other settlers' replies. Lines advance every 18 active seconds and enter the camp record as they are spoken. Nearby hostiles prevent a gathering or pause an ongoing conversation; guards can still fight. The evening ends at 21:00. Attendance is optional with no refusal penalty. Live subtitles do not replay missed lines; the shared camp record retains the conversation. There is no offline social simulation.

## Loss

A recruited settler who is downed has three active surface minutes to receive first aid. One bandage stabilizes them and begins the existing slow recovery. The countdown is shown in management and over the injured resident. An unrescued settler dies permanently; normal movement, aid and spawning cannot resurrect them. The unrecruited rescue NPC recovery rule is unchanged.

All remaining settlers lose 15 morale and mourn the named person for three active game days. Mourning reduces the morale target by 18, work speed by 20%, and belonging growth by half. Conversation and greetings acknowledge the loss. Further losses refresh rather than endlessly stack the duration. A named memorial with a flower appears at camp, and the memory book retains the loss after the temporary debuff ends. A quiet vigil at camp can grant survivors 2 morale once per day per lost person; it does not shorten grief.

All social state belongs to the world and is saved and replicated: arrival order, acceptance, grief, stories, spoken lines, meal consumption and memorials. Multiplayer protocol 38 requires everyone to update together.

Checks cover physical first aid and death, non-resurrection, neutral arrivals and gradual acceptance, missing food/absence, scheduled gathering and weekly deduplication, threat pauses, comfort cooldowns, save serialization and real guest RPCs for dialogue, shared history and mourning.
