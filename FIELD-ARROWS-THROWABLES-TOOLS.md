# Field kit progression

Open **F3**, or K > Field kit. Select an arrow type or throwable. **G** throws the selected item; select Stone distraction for the original stone toss. Empty throwable selections do not silently spend a different item. Select a harvesting tool, close the menu, then **T** equips it; aim and click for a timed strike. All new stocks and tool levels travel with the character.

## Arrows

All bows and crossbows use the selected payload. It is captured when fired and cannot be changed mid-flight. Bonuses are flat impact damage before enemy resistance; valid headshots double the bonus.

| Arrow | Craft tier | Extra impact damage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wood | 1 | 0 |
| Flint | 1 | 4 |
| Bone | 1 | 7 |
| Iron | 2 | 12 |
| Steel broadhead | 3 | 18 |
| Flaming | 2 | 6, plus 4 burning damage/second for 5 seconds |

Existing generic arrows remain wood arrows. The former stone-tipped arrow recipe now creates flint arrows, and the hunting bone-arrow recipe creates bone arrows.

## Throwables

| Item | Tier | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Crude Molotov | 1 | 3m ground fire for 6s; 6 damage/second, short lingering burn |
| Thickened Molotov | 3 | 4m ground fire for 12s; 14 damage/second, longer lingering burn |
| Makeshift nail jar | 2 | Up to 70 blast damage, 4m falloff radius |
| Military grenade | 4 | Up to 140 blast damage, 6m falloff radius |
| White/red/green/blue smoke | 2 | 18s concealment; identical 5m sight-blocking volumes |
| Flash grenade | 3 | 5s disruption to nearby ordinary hostiles; bosses resist with 0.6s disruption |
| Clockwork decoy | 2 | 20s distraction, repeated noise over a 45m radius |

Thrown items have physical flight and gravity. Explosive jars/grenades have a fuse and bounce. Fire and blast respect walls and can hurt players; smoke affects enemy perception rather than only drawing fog. Real attacks or new threats interrupt forced decoy return. Undisturbed infected investigate the device and return to their prior position after it expires. Passive story figures and bosses are not forcibly dragged away.

Glass containers and raw scrap are salvaged from cars. Plant-oil fuel is a hand-crafted early fuel option. All recipes use abstract game materials; higher tiers require research and workshops. T3+ choices retain the existing specialization limits.

## Tools

| Tool family | Progression | Harvest damage |
|---|---|---|
| Axe | Stone > iron | 35 > 60 |
| Pickaxe | Stone > iron | 25 > 50 |
| Mechanic | Screwdriver > wrench > blowtorch | 20 > 40 > 65 |
| Skinning knife | Flint > bone > iron | Faster skinning; unchanged yield ratio |

Skinning knives still produce 200% hides/fur and 50% meat/bone. Knife levels reduce harvest time from 3s to approximately 2.31s and 1.88s. Cleaver harvesting retains the opposite yield choice.

Trees retain strength requirements and saved felling. Rocks require an aimed pickaxe strike; loose starting stones can still be collected by hand. Mechanic tools dismantle an aimed car rather than granting scrap merely by standing nearby. Blowtorch strikes consume fuel. Each wreck can pay out only once. Iron and other metal weapons now require raw scrap where appropriate; higher-tier alloy remains separate.

## Flamethrower

The salvaged flamethrower is a Tier 3 Ballistics weapon, crafted at workshop 3. Hold fire for short fuel-fed pulses with a 7m cone, direct heat damage and a lingering burn. It uses fuel rather than firearm ammunition; the normal gun level/exploration gate still applies.

## Multiplayer

Protocol 43 requires matching builds. Crafting, arrow selection, throwable costs, projectile hits, harvesting and damage are resolved by the host. Colored smoke/fire/decoy areas replicate to clients, including players joining while effects are active. Character ammo and material costs remain private. Temporary combat effects clear on room changes and are not permanent world objects.
