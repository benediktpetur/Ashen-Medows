# Hunting tools

Open K > Hunting. Craft either tool in tier 1 without a workbench:

- Flint skinning knife: 3 stone, 1 timber, 2 fiber.
- Stone butcher cleaver: 3 stone, 2 timber, 2 fiber.

Select your tool in Hunting, then press F beside a dead healthy animal and stay nearby for three seconds. Balanced hand harvesting remains available.

| Selection | Hide / fur | Raw meat / bone | Sinew |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hands | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Skinning knife | 200% | 50% | 100% |
| Butcher cleaver | 50% | 200% | 100% |

200% means twice the normal yield. Hide is the raw ingredient for tanning leather; animals do not drop finished leather. Tools change existing species yields, so a deer still does not produce fur. Half units accumulate in your portable character inventory and become usable whole units on later hunts. Hunting shows pending fractions and nearby carcass yield previews.

The server locks the selected tool when harvesting starts. Each carcass pays once; you cannot skin it and then butcher it for both bonuses. Tool ownership and rewards belong to the harvesting player. Tool models appear in the inventory preview and item catalogue. Co-op protocol 41 requires all players to update.
