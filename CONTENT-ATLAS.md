# The South Bank field atlas

The content expansion adds 28 authored locations to the existing southeast-to-northwest map. It preserves the primary stories, river boundary and bosses. Locations have seeded, irregular positions and reserved footprints. They load near active players, unload beyond 230m, and their meshes stop drawing beyond 220m. Roads gain signposts, mailboxes and small abandoned roadside details. This does not load the whole map at once.

Twelve layout themes provide farm beds, ranger-lodge furnishings, medical rooms, recovery garages, pump tanks, radio towers, railway infrastructure, shop stock, archive shelves, kitchens, industrial plant and checkpoint equipment. Raised foundations and approach ramps follow the terrain. Each site has its own written record and interaction point; loot and choices are persistent world state.

Press **F** at a site's record/case. Discovered sites appear on **M**. Open **F7 → Field guide** or the journal's field-guide button to review discovered records and requests.

## Activities and rewards

* **Supply caches:** clear tier-appropriate guardians and claim finite food, dressings, materials, arrows, fuel, water, typed ammunition or named field gloves. Meadow sites have no fixed guardians and give no firearms.
* **Cold-store generators:** spend two fuel and remain in the area for sixty active seconds. The generator makes real noise every eight seconds; nearby infected can investigate. The timer pauses when no player is nearby. Clear the guardians before claiming the case.
* **Pump and signal repairs:** clear the site, then spend six timber at the meadow station or six alloy at later stations to restore its supply compartment.
* **Night receivers:** the cache unlocks only at night, with stronger night guardians and useful night materials. A cleared receiver still requires darkness to claim.
* **Rest stops:** clear threats, spend four timber, and gain twenty energy, five morale and five minutes of comfort. Ten-minute active cooldown. Rest does not instantly restore health, and hostiles within twenty metres prevent it.

The new site guardians use existing regional infected behavior, hit reactions and progression rules. Dead guardians and claimed cases do not reset when a location streams out or the world is reloaded.

View field requests from the field guide or a resident's reputation page. Accept and return them while standing at the relevant settlement. Acceptance pins the location; ordinary supply runs have no hidden deadline. You may recover a case before hearing its request, then accept and deliver later.

At the destination, choose to reserve the sealed case for the community or break it open for extra personal supplies. Keeping it pays immediately, costs eight personal reputation and four shared trust, and makes that request impossible to finish. Delivering pays personal materials, dressings, career XP and a small research award within the existing lifetime limit; it also adds four food and four water to the settlement and improves relationships. A dead requester or evacuated/hostile settlement cannot complete a request. Side jobs maintain communities but cannot resurrect residents or reverse a failed main storyline.

Cases are world-owned shared quest state, rather than transferable character equipment. Only one delivery/reward occurs per request, even in co-op.

## The Names We Keep

Read records at six, fourteen and all twenty-eight sites, then claim milestones in the field guide. Six records give provisions and dressings; fourteen give the **South Bank courier coat**; twenty-eight give the **Ashen cartographer hood**. Rewards and XP are claimed once per character and seed. They do not grant legendary boss upgrades.

Ordinary dungeons now have six seeded dressing themes: flooded maintenance, makeshift ward, sealed records, emergency stores, root cellar and research annex. Each has distinct signs, pipes/fixtures and lighting. A courier-seal cache near the entrance opens after six field records and after clearing nearby infected. It gives zone-tier materials and a medical kit once per dungeon/world. The family basement and final boss arena retain their own authored layouts.

## Roaming events

After active play reaches woodland or harder country, one new event can occur at a time, with fifteen-to-twenty-two-minute cooldowns after resolution. They stay out of the starting meadow and are marked on the map/HUD. Their active timers pause when no player remains near the event. Existing drone events remain separate and can overlap.

* **A horde on the move:** four woodland or six later-zone infected migrate across the area toward a route destination. You can avoid them, follow them, intercept them or draw them away with noise. Disturbed infected remain after the event ends.
* **Hettie's trading cart:** a four-minute stop with one transaction of limited stock. Three food buys two bandages and two fuel; in T3+ country eight alloy buys rifle and carbine ammunition. The trader has health and ordinary infected can kill her.
* **Owen's last delivery:** an injured adult courier needs two bandages and one food within three active minutes. Helping him gives spare electronics, reveals an unexplored destination and brings supplies/reputation to a surviving friendly settlement. Taking his supplies instead ends the rescue and costs reputation. Ordinary infected can also kill him before you help.

All sites, jobs, event state and outcomes belong to the host world; loot and payments belong to the acting character. **Multiplayer protocol 28: all players must update together.**
