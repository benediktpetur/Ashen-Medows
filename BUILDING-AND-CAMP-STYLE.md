# Building and camp pass

B opens a dedicated placement catalogue: Structure, Interiors, Defenses and Survival. Known blueprints remain selectable even when you cannot yet afford them; costs and the missing requirement are shown. Research has its own button.

Snap connects rotated foundations face to face, places walls on deck edges and centers roofs over the supporting room. G disables snapping for free positioning, Q/E rotates in 15-degree steps, Ctrl+Q/E uses one degree, Page Up/Down adjusts height, +/- changes reach and R resets rotation/height. The preview shows the actual piece, with green/red validation. Existing support, collision, load and protected-story rules still apply.

## Material progression

- T1: rough salvaged timber, plank seams, nails and braces. A foundation, two walls and a roof cost 13 timber, 2 stone, 4 fiber. The initial finite forage alone provides 60 timber, 48 stone, 48 fiber, before existing starter piles and trees. Tools, cot, storage and survival facilities still require their own materials.
- T2: stone base courses with timber bracing. Structural pieces use stone and woodland resin.
- T3: riveted scrap steel with rusted ribs, requiring highway alloy scrap.
- T4: reinforced concrete, steel and city electronics.
- T5: olive military armor panels, rivets, identification plates and warning stripes. Alloy, electronics and bioalloy are required.

Eight appended buildables, including a full-width cabin doorway with a human-sized hinged opening: salvaged table, camp stool, patchwork rug, keepsake shelf, resin lantern, scrap workshop table and military footlocker. Basic wooden decorations are known from the start and cost materials, rather than limited research points. Lanterns require T2 research/materials and give a small warm light; they do not purify water or grant a zombie-proof safe zone. Advanced furnishing and military storage retain normal research requirements.

Existing blueprint indices, saved placement and research IDs are retained. Multiplayer protocol is 37: all players must update together. This pass does not relocate existing settlements or implement a new fixed-settlement claim system. Vehicles remain unchanged.

Validation includes real rotated connections/support, roof alignment, a starter material budget, tier resource gates and rendered decoration geometry, plus the existing complete gameplay and two-process co-op checks.
