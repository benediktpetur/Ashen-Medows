# ASHEN MEADOW
## Leave safety. Bring something back.

Promotional copy draft · 16 October 2026

### The pitch

The meadow is where you catch your breath. The city is where you test your nerve.

Ashen Meadow is a first-person survival game about venturing deeper into an infected world—and building a place worth returning to. Gather supplies, craft better equipment, establish shelters, and follow the trail from open grassland through woodland and ruined streets into the depths below the city.

Play alone or bring friends. Every expedition asks the same question: how much further can you go before it is time to turn back?

### Short description

Venture from a quiet meadow into infected forests, ruined streets, and underground wards. Scavenge, craft, build, and grow through the things you do. Survive alone or with friends, manage wounds and dwindling supplies, and make every return to camp count.

### Feature copy

- **Push beyond the meadow.** Travel through increasingly dangerous regions, with better materials and equipment drawing you deeper into the ruins.
- **Earn your abilities through action.** Train your strength by chopping, your endurance by moving, and your marksmanship by surviving fights.
- **Prepare for the journey home.** Hunger, thirst, fatigue, and persistent wounds make preparation part of survival. Bandages stop bleeding; recovery takes time.
- **Make your mark.** Build supported shelters, fell trees, clear paths, dig wells, and establish sleeping-bag camps in a world that remembers your changes.
- **Search beneath the city.** Explore seven dungeon entrances, guarded loot, and deeper wards where the best discoveries demand more preparation.
- **Share the expedition.** Host a direct-connect co-op world with up to seven friends. Explore the surface independently and regroup for dungeon expeditions.
- **Read what remains.** Abandoned camps, checkpoints, belongings, and recovered notes reveal fragments of the people who came before you.

### Playtest announcement

**A quiet meadow. A dangerous road. A reason to come back.**

We’re building Ashen Meadow, a first-person survival game where every trip beyond camp is a decision: what to carry, where to explore, and when to turn back.

The current development build brings together scavenging, crafting, structural building, activity-based skills, persistent wounds, seven dungeon entrances, and direct-connect co-op. Our next focus is making that journey clearer, more dependable, and more rewarding through playtesting.

We want feedback on the first hour, combat, progression, and surviving together. Your stories from the road will help shape what comes next.

Our direction for Ashen Meadow: strengthen the first hour, make survival and co-op dependable, deepen expeditions, and bring the world’s illustrated identity into sharper focus. Beyond that: communities, bosses, vehicles, and the far side of the river are ideas we want to explore.
