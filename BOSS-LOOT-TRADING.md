# Boss kill-group loot trading

New boss equipment remains bound to its original kill group. Each item saves a kill ID and the portable character IDs eligible at victory. Trading changes its owner, preserving the exact roll and original group; receiving it never adds anyone to that group. There is no item trade deadline.

Inventory > Soulbound armory > Trade with your boss kill group. Stand within 6m, unequip or uninstall the item, select a recipient, and ask them to accept through the same screen. Offers expire after 60 real seconds and disappear on server restart. Refresh updates the list; acceptance checks distance, living characters, ownership, eligibility and equipped state again. It transfers one item, not the character's other rewards. This is a consensual gift flow, not simultaneous item barter.

Applies to new Foreman/campaign armor, Broodheart Soulbound weapons, mega-boss weapons, armor, individual weapon upgrades, camp modules and companion harnesses. Stackable currencies/materials and research points stay personal. Previously earned items have no reliable kill roster and remain bound.

The original nearby victory roster is captured using each encounter's reward radius (mega hunts require living players within 65m). Existing participant rewards remain available, but absent participants are not added as recipients to other players' items. Eligibility persists with the item across saves and servers. Co-op protocol 42 requires matching builds.

Verified with isolated tests for all five item families and real two-process ENet offer/accept/return tests.
