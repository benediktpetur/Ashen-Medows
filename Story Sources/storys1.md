## Story 1

**Title:** The Last Survivors

**Characters:**
- **John Doe** (35)
- **Jane Smith** (30)

**Setting:** A small town in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

John and Jane were the last survivors in their small town. They had been hiding in their basement for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. John and Jane welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the town and use it as their own. John and Jane had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the town was saved.

## Story 2

**Title:** The Lost Boy

**Characters:**
- **Tommy** (12)

**Setting:** A deserted city in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

Tommy was a lost boy in a deserted city. He had been wandering for days, trying to find his way back home. One day, he stumbled upon a group of survivors. They welcomed him in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to use Tommy to find his way back home and use it as their own. Tommy had to fight for his life and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, he emerged victorious and the city was saved.
