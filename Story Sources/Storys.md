# Side Stories of the Zombie Apocalypse

## Side Story 1

**Title:** The Last Survivors

**Age:** 25

**Character:** Sarah

**Location:** Abandoned Hospital

**Story:** Sarah and her family were the last survivors in their town. They had been hiding in the abandoned hospital for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, Sarah's brother, who had been out looking for supplies, came back to the hospital. He was in a bad shape, but he was alive. Sarah and her family were overjoyed, but they knew that they had to be careful. They had to make sure that the zombies didn't find them. They spent the next few days trying to find a way out of the hospital, but they were constantly being attacked by zombies. Finally, they found a way out and left the hospital, never to return.

## Side Story 2

**Title:** The Lost Boy

**Age:** 10

**Character:** Tim

**Location:** Abandoned School

**Story:** Tim was a lost boy in the zombie apocalypse. He had been wandering around for days, trying to find a safe place to stay. He had seen many zombies, but he had never seen a human. One day, he came across an abandoned school. He thought it might be a safe place, so he went inside. He found a group of survivors, and they welcomed him with open arms. Tim was happy to be with them, but he knew that he had to be careful. He had to make sure that the zombies didn't find them. He spent the next few days helping the survivors, and he became a valuable member of the group.

## Story 3: The Betrayal

**Character:** Emily, 25

**Location:** Abandoned School

Emily was a teacher before the zombie apocalypse. She had always loved children and wanted to make a difference in their lives. When the zombies came, she knew she had to do something to help. She went to the abandoned school and started teaching the survivors. She was a great teacher, but she had a secret. She had been working with a group of zombies, trying to find a cure for the virus. She was betrayed by her own kind and was turned into a zombie.
