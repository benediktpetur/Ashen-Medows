## Story 5

**Title:** The Abandoned Hospital

**Characters:**
- **Dr. James** (50)
- **Nurse Sarah** (45)

**Setting:** An abandoned hospital in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

Dr. James and Nurse Sarah were the last survivors in an abandoned hospital. They had been hiding in the hospital for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. Dr. James and Nurse Sarah welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the hospital and use it as their own. Dr. James and Nurse Sarah had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the hospital was saved.
