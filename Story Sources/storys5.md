## Story 9

**Title:** The Abandoned Hotel

**Characters:**
- **Hotel Manager** (40)
- **Receptionist** (30)

**Setting:** An abandoned hotel in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

The Hotel Manager and Receptionist were the last survivors in an abandoned hotel. They had been hiding in the hotel for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. The Hotel Manager and Receptionist welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the hotel and use it as their own. The Hotel Manager and Receptionist had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the hotel was saved.
