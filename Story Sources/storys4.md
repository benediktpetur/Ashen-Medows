## Story 7

**Title:** The Abandoned Warehouse

**Characters:**
- **Alex** (25)
- **Jordan** (28)

**Setting:** An abandoned warehouse in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

Alex and Jordan were the last survivors in an abandoned warehouse. They had been hiding in the warehouse for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. Alex and Jordan welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the warehouse and use it as their own. Alex and Jordan had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the warehouse was saved.
