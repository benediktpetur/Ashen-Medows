## Story 3

**Title:** The Forgotten Village

**Characters:**
- **Lucas** (40)
- **Sophia** (35)

**Setting:** A forgotten village in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

Lucas and Sophia were the last survivors in a forgotten village. They had been hiding in their house for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. Lucas and Sophia welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the village and use it as their own. Lucas and Sophia had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the village was saved.
