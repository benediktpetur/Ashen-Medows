## Story 11

**Title:** The Abandoned School

**Characters:**
- **Principal** (50)
- **Teacher** (40)

**Setting:** An abandoned school in the middle of a zombie apocalypse.

**Story:**

The Principal and Teacher were the last survivors in an abandoned school. They had been hiding in the school for weeks, waiting for the zombies to pass. One day, they heard a knock at the door. It was a group of survivors looking for shelter. The Principal and Teacher welcomed them in, but soon realized that they were not as kind as they seemed. They were planning to take over the school and use it as their own. The Principal and Teacher had to fight for their lives and the lives of the other survivors. In the end, they emerged victorious and the school was saved.
