# Survivor records

All 17 stories from `Storys.md` (file 0) and `storys1.md` through `storys6.md` are included as recoverable survivor records. Original source files are retained in `Story Sources/`.

Search the papers at abandoned story sites with E. Each site's record is selected deterministically from the world seed and site identity. Reopen recovered records from F7 / Journal; numbered labels distinguish repeated titles. Text, character names, ages and settings are preserved. These are readable accounts, not new NPC quests or combat encounters.

Existing dungeon records and wreck letters remain available. Recovery uses the existing saved journal, research award deduplication and authoritative co-op letter delivery. No save migration is required.
